## Suggested FAQ / body copy

### How it works (short)

We build a preview on our URL. If you like it, you can either pay the $2,000 in full, or put $500 down to move that same preview live onto your domain and Cloudflare as-is. The remaining balance completes the build and unlocks 30 days of included revisions (starting when you send your first feedback). After that, you own everything — or add optional managed hosting.

### What’s the $500 deposit for?

It lets you go live on your real domain with the approved preview before you’re ready to do a full round of feedback. No revision work is included at the deposit stage (except critical fixes). The $500 counts toward the $2,000. The leftover $1,500 unlocks the 30-day revision window.

### When does the 30-day revision window start?

After you’ve paid the full $2,000. It starts when you send your first revision request — then you get 30 consecutive days. Take your time gathering photos and notes; the clock doesn’t start until you’re ready to give feedback.

### Can I skip the deposit?

Yes. Pay $2,000 once. We put the site on your domain, move hosting into your Cloudflare, and your revision window follows the same rules.

### What if I only pay the deposit and never finish?

You’ve paid to go live with the approved preview as-is. Revision rounds and the rest of the handoff package (GBP push, full docs, etc.) wait on the remaining balance. We don’t treat the deposit as a free forever revision retainer.
